## 🖼️ How to Edit Your First Photo

Now for the fun part! Let's turn that plain picture into a masterpiece.

1.  **Open rawmakase:** Launch the app like you did in the previous step.
2.  **Import Your Photo:** Look for a button that says "Import" or "Open" and click it. Find the RAW photo on your computer and select it. It will appear in the main window.
3.  **Start Editing:** You will see a panel on the right side with sliders and tools. Here are the most common ones:
    - **Exposure:** This is how bright or dark your photo is. Drag the slider to the right to brighten, or left to darken.
    - **Contrast:** This makes the dark parts darker and the light parts lighter, adding punch to your image.
    - **Warmth:** This changes the color temperature. Slide right for warmer (orange) tones, or left for cooler (blue) tones.
    - **Crop:** If you want to cut out parts of the photo, click the crop tool, draw a box, and press Enter.
4.  **See the Result:** You will see your changes instantly. That is the magic of rawmakase—it is fast and responsive.
5.  **Save Your Work:** When you are happy, click the "Export" or "Save" button. You can choose the format (like JPG) and quality. Your finished photo will be saved to your computer.
